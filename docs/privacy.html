Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 27, 2026  |  Last Updated: April 27, 2026  |  App Version: 1.0+

1. Introduction

PushGram ("we", "us", or "our") is a fitness-based screen-time management application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user. By using PushGram, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the app's core features:

  • Camera data: Used in real-time to count push-ups, squats, and crunches using on-device pose detection (ML Kit). No video or images are ever stored or transmitted.
  • Account information: Username, email address, and encrypted password provided during registration.
  • Workout data: Rep counts, exercise type, session duration, XP, and form quality — stored locally and optionally synced to Firebase.
  • Usage data: App interaction events for crash reporting and performance improvement. No browsing history or third-party app usage is tracked.
  • Accessibility service data: PushGram uses an Accessibility Service solely to detect when Instagram is opened, enabling credit-based screen-time enforcement. We do not read, record, or transmit any content from Instagram or any other app.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To count exercise reps and award credits using on-device camera processing
  • To maintain your workout history, XP, rank, and leaderboard position
  • To enforce your personal screen-time credit limits
  • To sync your profile across devices via Firebase (if you create an account)
  • To improve app stability through anonymised crash reports

4. Camera & Pose Detection

All camera processing happens entirely on your device. Pose estimation is performed by Google ML Kit locally. No images, video frames, or biometric data are ever uploaded, stored on servers, or shared with any third party.

5. Accessibility Service

PushGram's Accessibility Service detects only window state changes to identify when the Instagram app is in the foreground. It does not read screen content, passwords, messages, or any personal data from Instagram or any other application.

6. Data Storage & Security

  • Workout data and credits are stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences
  • If you use online features, data is synced to Firebase (Google) with encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • Passwords are hashed — we never store plaintext credentials
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party

7. Third-Party Services

PushGram integrates with the following services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, and crash reporting — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google ML Kit: On-device pose detection — developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms
  • YouTube Data API: Music import feature — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Spotify / Apple Music: Optional music integration — governed by your existing account with those services

8. Children's Privacy

PushGram is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Export your workout history at any time from the app
  • Opt-out: Disable Firebase sync in Settings to keep all data local only

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. We will notify you of significant changes through an in-app notification. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
